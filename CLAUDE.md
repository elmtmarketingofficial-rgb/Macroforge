# MacroForge

## Read `ROADMAP.md` before you plan anything

`ROADMAP.md` is the source of truth for **what gets built next**, in what
order, and which gates are closed. It carries the phase order, the
constraints that are not open to renegotiation, and the one rule that
overrides everything else (nothing gets monetised until activation is proven).

If what you find on disk or in production contradicts the roadmap, **the disk
wins and the roadmap is wrong — so fix the roadmap in this session**, dated,
before you build on top of it. Do not work around a stale plan and do not
leave the correction for someone else: this project has already lost days to
a document that said a live feature was dark.

Where the roadmap is silent, the decision has not been made. Take it to Ethan
and add it under "Open questions" rather than choosing for him.

Also in this repo: `PROMPT-CLAUDE-CODE.md` (how to start an engineering
session), `HANDOFF.md` (product and marketing detail), `RESEARCH.md`
(competitors), `TESTER-NOTES.md` (what testers have and have not been told).
