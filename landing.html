Why it's different
🛒

Ingredients first

Your grocery list and pantry generate the week's meal plan — aimed at your targets, honest about what's missing. Food first, plan second. The way it actually works.

📷

The store scanner

Scan any barcode in the aisle. GOOD PICK or SKIP IT — judged against your remaining week and a whole-foods hierarchy, not a generic health score.

🎯

A coach that measures you

Your maintenance calories, measured from your own logs and weigh-ins — not a formula's guess. Targets adapt as your body does.

🔔

Reminders that fit your day

Set how many meals you eat and when. MacroForge nudges you at meal time — and again if you forgot to log one.

🏋️

Training built in

Routines with your last session's numbers ghosted in, auto rest timers, PR detection, strength history per exercise.

🔒

Private by design

No account. No password. Your data lives on your device; link phone and desktop with a private sync code you control. Delete the synced copy any time.
